# Default post benchmark

Source: user-provided `小红书数据指标分析.pdf`. Use a newer benchmark when the user provides one. These are working reference ranges, not official RedNote distribution guarantees.

| Metric | Low / concern | Middle / qualified | High / strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure | 0–500 | 500–20,000 | 20,000+ |
| Cover click-through | <5% | 5%–15% | 15%+ |
| 2-second exit | >60% | 30%–60% | <30% (lower is better) |
| 5-second completion | <20% | 20%–30% | 40%–50% strong; 60%+ particularly strong |
| Engagement rate | <8% | 8%–15% | 15%+ |
| Follower growth rate | <2% | 2%–5% | 5%+ |

The source's overview calls 40%+ 5-second completion high, while its detail page describes 40% as medium, 40%–50% as excellent, and 60%+ as showing viral potential. It leaves some intervals unlabeled. Describe boundary cases plainly instead of forcing a rigid grade. It describes 20%–50% homepage recommendation share as a typical range, but that is context, not a pass/fail gate. Full-video completion depends on video length and format; the PDF provides no universal threshold for it.

Only grade a rate when its platform definition and denominator are clear. A follower count is not a follower growth rate. Traffic-source percentages are shares of views, not proof of algorithmic preference.
